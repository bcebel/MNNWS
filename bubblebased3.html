The Math of a "Chill" Social Network: How BubbleBased Uses P2P to Flip the Creator Economy

Part 3 of our ongoing exploration of BubbleBased and decentralized social networks.

Can a social network win simply by being... chill?

Every major web platform today operates on an aggressive value model: serve heavy media via multi-million dollar AWS CDNs, extract your behavioral data to pay off those server bills, and deploy hyper-optimized algorithms designed to trap your eyeballs in comment wars. You aren't just the user—you become the product.

To understand why BubbleBased is taking a different path, it helps to look through the lens of Curt Carlson's famous SRI value creation model: N = A x B/C x Comp (Need = Approach x Benefit/Cost x Competition).

1. The Need: Escaping the Attention Trap

The core Need isn't just "another app." It’s a refuge from hyper-monetized rage-bait feeds. Everyone already pays monthly for high-speed Wi-Fi, 5G cellular plans, and powerful mobile hardware. Yet, despite having incredible infrastructure and AI tools at our fingertips, we are forced into digital panopticons where randos derail conversations in public comment sections.

What if social media wasn't an aggressive algorithm designed to outrage you, but simply a place where friends and family hang out? What if privacy was built into digital "Bubbles" where context matters?

2. The Approach: WebTorrent, Local Caching, and Peer Swarms

To serve media without turning users into data products, the Approach must bypass centralized cloud servers. Serving video and high-res imagery costs an arm and a leg. BubbleBased solves this by pairing social groups directly with WebTorrent and local peer caching.

"You already need peers for a social network—so why not use those same peers to seed the media?"

When you view a video or image gallery in a Bubble, your browser temporarily holds those chunks and helps seed them to the next active member. The platform leverages infrastructure users already pay for, drastically bending cloud egress costs down to near zero.

3. Benefit / Cost & Competition: 1-on-1 Moderated Invites

The Benefit is clear: zero ads harvesting your profile, complete ownership over your content, and a private room where you can actually be yourself. The Competition is stuck trying to maximize screen time to pay off server bills.

To test this model safely, BubbleBased is rolling out a 1-on-1 trusted invitation model: moderators invite trusted moderators, who in turn invite chill people. The goal isn't 10 million random users shouting into the void tomorrow—it's creating the coolest, most reliable digital spaces possible, one room at a time. (Pro-tip: Don't get in a Bubble with randos!)

🔮 What's Next on the Roadmap

The current stack (Expo, React Native Web, GraphQL WebSockets, and WebTorrent) is just the baseline layer. Longer-term plans on the development roadmap focus on pushing peer-to-peer limits even further:

  • IPFS Ephemeral Hosting: Integrating IPFS as an additional fallback data layer for ephemeral file availability when swarm peer counts are low.
  • Advanced Swarm Caching: Intelligently pre-fetching media chunks across local Wi-Fi networks to make gallery swiping instant.
  • Community Revenue Sharing: Letting creators attach their own affiliate links directly to profiles rather than the platform taking a cut.

A "chill" social media app works because it respects both your bandwidth and your mental health. By changing the underlying network topology from centralized servers to peer swarms, BubbleBased proves that social media doesn't have to be noisy to be effective.
